# CodeAlpha_DataViz_IPL 🏏

## Task 3: Data Visualization — CodeAlpha Data Analytics Internship

### 📌 Objective
Transform raw IPL match data (2008-2020) into meaningful visual insights covering team performance, toss impact, venues, and player achievements.

### 🛠 Tools & Libraries Used
- Python
- Pandas, NumPy
- Matplotlib, Seaborn

### 🌐 Data Source
IPL Complete Dataset (2008-2020) — Kaggle

### 🔍 Process
1. Cleaned and standardized team names across seasons (handling renamed franchises).
2. Analyzed total wins by team across all seasons.
3. Tracked number of matches played per season.
4. Studied the impact of winning the toss on match outcomes.
5. Identified top 10 players with most "Player of the Match" awards.
6. Found top 10 venues by matches hosted.
7. Analyzed toss decision trends (bat vs field first).

### 📊 Key Insights
- Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings rank among the most successful teams by total wins.
- Winning the toss does not strongly guarantee a match win — outcome is fairly balanced.
- A small set of star players dominate "Player of the Match" awards.
- Eden Gardens and Wankhede Stadium are among the most-used IPL venues.
- Teams increasingly choose to field first after winning the toss, likely due to dew and chasing advantages.

### 📁 Files
- `matches.csv` — Dataset
- `team_wins.png` — Total wins by team
- `matches_per_season.png` — Matches played per season
- `toss_impact.png` — Toss winner vs match winner
- `top_players.png` — Top 10 Player of the Match winners
- `top_venues.png` — Top 10 venues by matches hosted
- `toss_decision_trend.png` — Bat vs Field first trend
